## Scope

PR #216 implements and documents the Issue #54 study of multi-seed MoT routing stability, evidence integrity, and
architecture controls. It combines research infrastructure, formal experiments, compatibility fixes, tests, and
reports in the contributor's fork branch.

## Main implementation

- Manifest schemas, registry, and checkpoint SHA256 validation.
- Safeguards against duplicate checkpoints masquerading as independent seeds.
- MoT routing export and cross-seed alignment.
- Dominant/token/JSD/entropy/utilization metrics.
- Precision calibration, isolated Phase 3 runners, and strict serial queues.
- EsMoE and MoA architecture controls kept outside MoT routing claims.
- Cross-version mixture compatibility and real MoT exploration gradients.
- Formal reports, checksum records, tests, and PR visuals.
